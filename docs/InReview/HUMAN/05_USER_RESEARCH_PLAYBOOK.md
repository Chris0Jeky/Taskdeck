# User Research Playbook (practical, low-cost)

## Objective
Validate whether “board maintenance overhead” is a strong enough pain that users will adopt a new system.

## Your first 10 interviews (who to recruit)
- developers who already use any task system (Trello/Notion/TODO files)
- students who struggle with follow-through
- people who take meeting notes and lose tasks

## Avoid fake demand
Do not ask:
- “would you use this?”
Ask:
- “what do you do today?”
- “what’s the last time you dropped the system?”
- “what did it cost you?”

## Interview script (short)
1) “Walk me through the last time you had too many tasks to keep in your head.”
2) “What system did you use? Why that system?”
3) “When did it break down? What was the friction?”
4) “How much time do you spend maintaining it per week?”
5) “If maintenance was automatic, what would you want it to do?”
6) “What would make you NOT trust automation?”

## Usability tests (watch them use it)
Give 3 tasks:
1) capture 3 messy notes quickly
2) triage them into board tasks with proposals
3) edit/approve and apply

Record:
- where they hesitate
- what they misclick
- how long to complete
- what they expect vs what happens

## What to measure early
- time-to-capture
- time-to-organize
- frequency of use after 1 week
- whether they keep capturing (habit formation signal)

## What this is
A consolidation of:
- Your original product motivation: *Kanban helps, but maintenance overhead causes attrition.*
- The current Taskdeck architecture: local-first + proposal-first automation + keyboard-first UX.
- A concrete path to make the product feel like: **“a board that maintains itself.”**

## What you should do with this
1. Read `01_PRODUCT_THESIS.md` and decide if you want to commit to this thesis.
2. Read `03_EXECUTION_ROADMAP.md` and adopt the step-by-step plan.
3. Use `04_GTM_AND_MARKETING.md` and `05_USER_RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` to validate demand early.
4. Then use the archived `REPO_PACK` materials to seed or cross-check issues when needed.

## Key principle
The differentiator is not “AI features.”
The differentiator is: **capture is nearly free** and **automation is safe** (review-first, provenance, audit trail).

If the product does not feel *easier than doing nothing*, it will not retain you or other users.
